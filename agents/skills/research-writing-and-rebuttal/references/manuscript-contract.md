# Manuscript and rebuttal checklist

Check claim-to-evidence links, citation verification, figure/table/number consistency, method reproducibility, limitations, data/code availability, ethics, conflicts, funding, CRediT roles, and venue-specific AI-use requirements.

For each rebuttal item, point to a concrete manuscript change or explain why no change is made. Preserve polite, direct wording and do not claim unperformed work.
