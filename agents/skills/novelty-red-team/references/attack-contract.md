# Novelty attack contract

For each closest work capture stable citation ID, relationship type, exact overlapping claim/method/result, source locator, risk `low|medium|high`, and required response. The response may be cite, compare, narrow, or pivot; it must never be “ignore”.

The memo ends with prohibited phrases until evidence changes, such as “first”, “novel”, “unexplored”, “outperforms”, or “SOTA”.
