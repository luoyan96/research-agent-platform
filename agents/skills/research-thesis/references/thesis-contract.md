# Thesis contract

`research-thesis.md` contains: problem, central claim C0, supporting claims, intended audience, named strongest neighbors, differentiator, scope, falsifier, primary metric, comparator, minimum meaningful effect, constraints, scorecard, decision, and next gate.

`claim-register.json` gives every claim an ID, type, exact wording, scope, evidence needed, current evidence links, falsifier, and status. A claim without `evidence_needed` is invalid.
